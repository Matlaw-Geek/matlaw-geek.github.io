Goodbye 70's, that is, minor visual changes and a new role for the "Event viewer"

9 August 2026

I don't know why I held off for so long, but I finally decided to make the buttons on the website look like buttons. Hooray!
In addition to the buttons, I've improved the navigation bar, which no longer consists of such narrow hyperlinks. Both of these changes will significantly improve the browsing experience on touchscreens.
...Or for those whose mice or/and trackballs are too sensitive.

The "Event viewer", which is where posts like this one are found, will now include information about which articles are new on the site. And yes, I also don't know why such an obvious idea only just occurred to me, but as they say - "better late than never".
This also means that the titles of future posts will no longer include those unfunny references to Yazoo song titles. Don't worry, the text in the website's footer is here to stay :)
