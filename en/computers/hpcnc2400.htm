HP Compaq nc2400

A small, but high-end laptop.
Representative of the HP Compaq series, which was created for the business market after Compaq was acquired by Hewlett-Packard.

This unit was once used in a motorcycle workshop, which caused damage to the paint on the lid from various fluids. The keyboard, RAM, disk, battery and cooling have been replaced.
