Links

Quite handy links and some others leading to my accounts in various services.

Software

  • Lynxed Cloud - My collection of software, drivers and more. Some of it is in Polish, but you'll figure it out.
  • My Internet Archive profile - Images of discs and other storage media, as well as media that I have managed to preserve.
  • PhilsComputerLab - Drivers (in different versions) and benchmarking software for old hardware.
  • OldVersion - Older versions of software for Windows.
  • Macintosh Garden - Software for older Macs.
  • Macintosh Repository - Another collection of software for older Macs. It often has copies from Macintosh Garden, but has download restrictions. However, it offers a more convenient way to discover programs.
  • The Apple Wiki - iTunes - Older versions of iTunes for Windows and Mac OS.
  • WinWorld - Installers of old operating systems and more.
  • PC OS Recovery Kits - Installers of old operating and OEM software for old computers.
  • LumiaFirmware - Firmware for Lumia devices.
  • Delta City '95 - A pack of all the released updates for Windows 95.
  • Supermium - Supermium browser (up-to-date Chrome for Windows XP) page on GitHub.

Useful and recommended

  • Piteusz Website - Interesting, real-life posts, a photo gallery and various files for old computers.
  • Polprog's site - Articles exploring the intricacies of electronics and programming.
  • The MacDat Network - Vintage hardware reference and repair information from around the web in one central place.
  • Legacy Portable Computing Wiki - Articles, specifications and interesting information about legacy mobile devices.
  • Repair Wiki - Repository of information for repairing any kind of device from phones to medical equipment.
  • Caps Wiki - Site dedicated to cataloging and sharing information on repairing devices.
  • Hardware Book [@web.archive.org] - A Collection of connector pinouts and cable descriptions.
  • Cornica - A must-visit website on an old Macintosh (Mac OS 7.5-9.2). It features QuickTime videos that play directly in the browser.
  • GUIdebook - A gallery of GUI elements of operating systems from 1982-2006.
  • Nathan's Toasty Technology page - A website in the style of the early days of the internet, featuring screenshots of many old operating systems and applications, articles, random files and tools.
  • VOGONS - A forum full of suggestions, advices and various things related to DOS.
  • Folklore - A diary packed with interesting facts and funny stories about the development of the first Macintosh.
  • The Cutting Room Floor - Articles, unused and cut content and interesting information about video games.
  • HMD Nokia Wiki - Articles and interesting information about Nokia-HMD devices.
  • endoflife.date - List of support dates for software and devices from various manufacturers.
